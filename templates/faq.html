Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the website, add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, fill in your details, and click "Complete Order via WhatsApp". Justin will receive your order instantly and confirm the final delivery fee with you!

Why is the delivery fee not shown at checkout?

To keep shoe prices as low as possible, we don't inflate prices to cover delivery. Instead, Justin calculates the exact boda-boda or courier fee based on your specific Kampala address after you order. No hidden fees!

What does "Available to Order" mean?

It means the shoe is currently with our supplier. Once you order, Justin physically goes to fetch it for you. This ensures you always get fresh stock and we don't waste money storing unsold shoes.

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes! We deliver across Central Uganda. Delivery to areas outside Kampala may take 2-3 days and incur a slightly higher courier fee, which will be confirmed via WhatsApp.
