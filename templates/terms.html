Terms & Conditions

1. Ordering Process

Because FootStyle operates as a mobile boutique to save you money, some items are marked "Available to Order". This means Justin will fetch the item from her supplier once you place your order via WhatsApp. This typically takes 1-2 business days.

2. Delivery Fees

Delivery fees are NOT fixed. Because distances in Central Uganda vary greatly (from Nakasero to Entebbe), Justin calculates the exact delivery fee based on your address after you checkout. You will confirm the final total (Subtotal + Delivery Fee) via WhatsApp before paying.

3. Payment Methods

We currently accept:

  • MTN Mobile Money (MoMo)
  • Airtel Money
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) - For trusted local customers.

4. Returns & Refunds

We want you to love your shoes! If there is a manufacturing defect or the size doesn't match what was ordered, Justin will exchange the item or refund your Mobile Money within 7 days. Items must be returned unworn and in original packaging.
